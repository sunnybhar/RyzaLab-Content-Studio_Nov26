# RyzaLab Training — Analytics — Content, Publication and Activity

Version: 2026-10-28 | Audience: All users within their permitted read scope

## Objective
Interpret real Studio metrics without confusing records with social engagement.

## Before you start
Create content and test planning/review flows first to generate meaningful records.

## Availability
Available in preview. Live operation requires the configured /studio workspace.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Open Analytics in the sidebar. Check the channel filter; metrics reflect records visible to your profile.
2. Read current inventory: Content items, In human review, Approved content, Published content, Planned publications and Open planning tasks. These inventory totals are all time.
3. Choose Activity period: Last 7, 30 or 90 days. Read items updated, approval snapshots and human publication confirmations for that rolling period.
4. Inspect the daily chart and View daily activity data. Daily grouping is labelled UTC. Approval counts are snapshots, so one content item can produce several approvals.
5. Read Content by channel. Approved/published content totals count unique content items, not every approval or repost task.
6. Review Next required checks to see where unpublished work is waiting. Review changes requested and blocked publication tasks.
7. Inspect AI usage and cost. Recorded actual cost requires reconciliation; reservations are budget holds, not confirmed charges.
8. Read Social performance. Views, impressions, clicks, followers and engagement are Not connected and must not be reported as zero performance.

## Expected result
Metrics change in line with actual records and filters; no social engagement is fabricated.

## Practice exercise
Create an editing task, check Open planning tasks, mark it done and verify the count decreases. Compare approval snapshots with unique approved content.

## Common problems and boundaries
Missing teammate records may be correct permission filtering. Published means a human supplied a URL, not independently verified reach. Deleted working content is excluded from current inventory.
