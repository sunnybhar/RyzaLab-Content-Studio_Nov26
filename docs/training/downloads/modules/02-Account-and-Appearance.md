# RyzaLab Training — Account and Appearance

Version: 2026-10-30 | Audience: All users

## Objective
Inspect your identity and choose an accessible theme, layout and navigation style.

## Before you start
Your workspace has loaded.

## Availability
Available in preview. Live operation requires the configured /studio workspace. Release 0.3.2: the two-decision flow requires explicit founder adoption; any legacy stage instructions below apply only before adoption. No paid provider or live-account acceptance is implied by these documents.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Click the initials avatar at the top right or the user control at the bottom of the sidebar. Both open Account & appearance.
2. Read Email, Assigned profiles and Workspace mode. In preview, the identity is a sample account; there is no live account to sign out.
3. Under Home layout, select Command Center or Production Board. Click Open selected layout to see it.
4. Under Color theme, choose Midnight Teal or Daylight Teal. Check that text, forms, calendar events and focus outlines remain readable.
5. Under Navigation, set Pin sidebar and Expand sidebar as preferred. Refresh and verify the choices remain.
6. In a live session, use Sign out on this page. In preview, Open live sign-in takes you to /studio; actual authentication depends on live configuration.

## Expected result
Both avatars open a useful account page; theme and layout choices visibly apply and persist.

## Practice exercise
Select Daylight Teal, refresh, return to Midnight Teal, and confirm your email/profile labels.

## Common problems and boundaries
Role changes are managed in People & access, not by clicking your own role label. Appearance changes do not grant permissions.
