# What Stays Home: campaign calendar (Oct 20, 2026 to Jan 31, 2027)

Source: RyzaLab Campaign Plan (Oct 9, 2026). **Draft until Ryan approves the campaign (decision D-01).** Hero titles are working titles: every piece still goes through the brand guard before publishing.

## How the weeks work

One hero piece a week carries the theme. Everything else is cut from it. About 20% of slots stay open for news (for example a big tech privacy story).

| Channel | Standing weekly cadence | Starts |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 3 Reels and 2 carousels | Oct 20 |
| TikTok | The same Reels, repurposed | Oct 20 |
| Facebook | Repurposed posts, plus helpful answers in local parent groups | Oct 20 |
| LinkedIn | 2 founder posts from Ryan, plus the newsletter as a LinkedIn newsletter | Oct 20 |
| YouTube | 1 long video a month, 2 Shorts a week cut from Reels | Nov 2 |
| Reddit and Home Assistant forum | 20 minutes a day of helpful answers. No links in the first month | Oct 20 |
| Newsletter (Kit, website, LinkedIn) | Every Tuesday from Oct 27 (cadence decision D-13) | Oct 27 |
| Google Business Profile | 1 post a week and a review request after every job | Oct 13 |
