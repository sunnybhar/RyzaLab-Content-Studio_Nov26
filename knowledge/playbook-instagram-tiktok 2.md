# Instagram + TikTok playbook

## The job of this channel

Awareness and trust with new and expecting parents, so they save a post, follow, grab the Private Nursery Checklist or book the free call. Instagram is the highest-effort channel in the plan and lower intent than search, so every piece must earn a save or a profile visit, not just a view.

Measure: saves and shares per post, profile visits, link-in-bio clicks, checklist signups, and the campaign's key number, **booked calls per 1,000 views**.

## Hooks (first 2 seconds, on-screen text)

Write 3 hook options per Reel. Patterns that work without breaking rules:
- A question the parent can't answer yet: "Where does your nursery camera send its video?"
- A specific relatable moment: "2 AM feed. Hallway light at full blast."
- A number from live prices or the proof library: "Five apps. One apartment."
- A contrast: "Same lights. One app. Runs inside the house."

Never use fear hooks about specific companies, and never imply danger to a child.

## Reel script rules

- 15 to 45 seconds. One idea.
- On-screen text carries the story with the sound off.
- Show the outcome in the first 3 seconds, explain after.
- End shot: the call to action on screen ("Free 15-minute call: link in bio").
- Add a blur list: anything in frame that identifies the home or a person.

Caption pattern: hook line under 125 characters, 2 to 4 short lines of value, one call to action, 3 to 5 hashtags.

## Hashtags (starting set, refresh monthly from what performs)

Pick 3 to 5 per post: #newparents #nycparents #brooklynmoms #brooklyndads #nurseryideas #smarthome #homeassistant #privacy #nycapartment #renterfriendly. Never use a hashtag to insert RyzaLab into a conversation it doesn't belong in.

## TikTok differences

- Export Reels without the Instagram watermark.
- Captions can be more conversational. Put the key words people search for in the on-screen text and caption.
- Business accounts use the Commercial Music Library. Give the editor a mood ("calm piano, low"), not a track name.
- Replies to comments as video replies work well for "how does that work?" questions.
