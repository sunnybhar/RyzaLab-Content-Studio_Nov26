# LinkedIn playbook

## The job of this channel

Ryan's credibility, conversations with NYC professionals, and recruiting referral partners (realtors, architects, interior designers). The Campaign plan rates it low effort and high fit. The CMO brief's rule applies most here: **a post should make a specific conversation easier**. Success is DMs, replies from the right people and partner meetings, not likes.

Measure: profile views from target roles, conversations started, newsletter subscribers, partner meetings booked.

## Post types

| Type | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| From the inside | A lesson from 15 years in data systems, applied to the home | "In risk work, you ask where data goes before you ask what a system does. I'd never done that for my own nursery camera." |
| Vendor risk lesson | A public event from the proof library, read as a GRC case | Nest Learning Thermostat support ended Oct 25, 2025 (P-04): what "you own the hardware" means when the service ends |
| Build log | What Ryan built, broke or fixed at home, and the recovery | The night an update broke a routine, and how the backup restored it |
| Trade post | For architects, designers, GCs: technology decisions that affect the drawings | "Shades need power and pockets. That's an architecture decision, not a gadget decision." |
| Behind the business | Honest notes on building RyzaLab | Why the Blueprint is paid, what the handoff includes |
| Document carousel | 5 to 8 slides, PDF, one idea per slide | The five layers of the privacy architecture |

Mix each week: one "inside" or "vendor risk" post, and one build log, trade or business post.

## Post structure

1. **Hook (first two lines, shown before "see more"):** a specific claim or moment, not a question to the void.
2. **Body:** 3 to 8 short paragraphs, one idea each. Concrete details over adjectives.
3. **Turn:** what Ryan changed or recommends.
4. **Close:** an honest prompt that invites the right people to talk ("If you're planning a renovation in Brooklyn next year, what's the tech decision you're least sure about?"). Link in the first comment if needed, with UTM `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social`.

Length: aim for 120 to 250 words. Hashtags: 0 to 3, only if they help.

## Partner recruiting posts

Speak peer to peer about coordination and timing (the trade one-pager): network coverage, lighting control points, shade power and pockets, access hardware, documentation at handoff. Call to action: "Bring RyzaLab in before the technology hardens: a project fit conversation." Never imply existing partnerships that don't exist.

## LinkedIn newsletter

Same issue as the Kit newsletter, with a LinkedIn-native title, a short intro line for the feed, and the call to action adjusted to "subscribe" plus the free call link.

## What not to post

- Fear about named companies or "big tech sells your data".
- Traction that doesn't exist ("growing fast", "clients across NYC").
- Anything about Ryan's other employer.
- The internal AI operating model as the selling point.
