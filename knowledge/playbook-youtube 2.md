# YouTube playbook

## The job of this channel

Two jobs: be found by people **searching** for a private smart home setup, and serve as **proof** that sales conversations can point to ("here's the offline demo"). Long video is high effort, so each one must keep working for months through search, and feed 6 to 10 Shorts and Reels.

Measure: search impressions and views from search, average view duration, subscribers, description link clicks, booked calls citing YouTube.

## Signature video structure (8 to 12 minutes)

| Section | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00 to 0:15 | The promise and a glimpse of the proof ("I'm going to unplug the router and run bedtime anyway") |
| Context | 0:15 to 1:30 | The problem in the viewer's words, and who this is for |
| The setup | 1:30 to 5:00 | What's installed and why, shown in the real home |
| The demo | 5:00 to 8:00 | The routine working; the offline test where relevant |
| Honest limits | 8:00 to 9:00 | What still needs a cloud service, what it costs, what can't be done |
| What it costs and how to start | 9:00 to 10:00 | Live prices only, hardware ranges from the FAQ |
| Call to action | last 20 seconds | Free 15-minute call, link in description |

Talking points, not a word-for-word script: Ryan sounds best speaking naturally. Write the hook and the call to action word for word.

## Titles

Search phrase first, promise second, under 100 characters. Patterns:
- "Private Smart Home Setup in a Brooklyn Apartment (No Cloud Cameras)" (only if true of the setup shown)
- "I Unplugged My Router. Here's What My Smart Home Still Does"
- "Checking on My Parents Without a Camera: Simple Sensor Setup"

## Thumbnails

Three concepts per video: a real frame from the home, 4 words or fewer, high contrast in Deep teal and Paper. No fake "shocked face", no red arrows at products, no child faces.

## Shorts

- 2 a week from Nov 2. Cut from Reels or a single moment of the signature video.
- One idea per Short. Shorts can run to 3 minutes, but keep each one as short as the idea allows, and compare lengths in the monthly review.
- First frame is the payoff. Title it like a search query.
- Return a cut list: source video, start and end time, on-screen text, title.
