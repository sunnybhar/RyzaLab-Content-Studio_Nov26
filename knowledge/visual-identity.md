# Visual identity (provisional)

Taken from the current RyzaLab one-pagers and ryzalab.com. Ryan's deck calls the visual system "provisional, pending QA", so treat this as the working standard until he approves a brand guide.

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep teal | #0B3A42 | Headlines, call-to-action bands, logo |
| Teal accent | #1F6E78 | Subheads, eyebrow labels, icons |
| Mist | #E4EFF2 | Highlight panels, table highlights |
| Paper | #F8F6EE | Page and slide background |
| Ink | #22302F | Body text |
| White | #FFFFFF | Text on deep teal |

Contrast: body text is Ink on Paper or White on Deep teal. Never teal accent text on Mist at small sizes.

## Type

- Headlines: Manrope, 700 (600 for subheads). Tight, sentence case, end with a period: "Design the home. Design how it works."
- Body: Inter, 400 and 500.
- Eyebrow labels: Inter 500, uppercase, wide letter spacing, Teal accent: "RESIDENTIAL TECHNOLOGY DESIGN + INTEGRATION".
- Logo: "RYZALAB" in wide-tracked capitals with a short teal rule beneath. Use the real logo file, never retype it in a design tool.

## Imagery

- Real homes, warm natural light, calm interiors. Ryan's own home is the main set.
- No faces of children or clients, no house numbers, street views, screens with personal data, Wi-Fi names or addresses. Blur anything that slips in.
- Client homes only with a signed consent form (Campaign plan, due Nov 30).
- Show the scene, not the hardware: the dimmed nursery, the amber path light, not a box of sensors.
- AI-generated or synthetic images are disclosed and never presented as a real RyzaLab install (CMO brief).

## Layout patterns from the one-pagers

- Big two-line headline, short subhead in Teal accent, one line of body.
- Two cards side by side: "INCLUDED" list and "IMAGINE" list.
- A three-column comparison table: capability, RyzaLab, typical setup. Comparisons describe setups, never named brands.
- A Deep teal band at the bottom with one call to action and ryzalab.com.
